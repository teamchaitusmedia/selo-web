Selo

The photo file manager used inside Chaitus Media.

Selo is a Windows app the Chaitus Media photography studio uses to browse, sort and rate the photos from its shoots, send files between the studio's own PCs, and play the occasional game of Ludo between them.

When a studio PC is away from the studio's network, Selo can reach the studio's other PCs over the internet. For that it signs in to the studio's own Google account and keeps a small address book of the studio's PCs in a hidden folder of that Google Drive. It never looks at any other file there. The details are in the privacy policy.

Selo is made for the studio's own staff and is not offered to the public.
